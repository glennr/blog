Medium: a technical line drawing in fine ink, like an engineer's sketch on
off-white drafting paper. Faint grid lines, hatching for shade, no fills.
Palette: ink #1F2A44 on paper #F4F1EA, with one accent, #E4572E, used on the
single element the post is about and nowhere else.
Composition: one central assembly of physical objects (pipes, valves, gears,
drawers, cables, brackets) that acts out the post's idea as a mechanism. Leave
generous empty paper around it. A few dimension lines and callout circles are
fine, but they point at shapes, never at words.
Mood: precise and a little dry, with one visual joke in the mechanism.
Avoid: any text, letters, numbers or labels; logos; faces or people; screens
and dashboards; glowing brains, circuit-board heads, robots, neon or sci-fi.
